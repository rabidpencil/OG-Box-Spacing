Walls

One run per clear span — a wall with a door in it is two runs. Outlets and switches are measured to a plate edge, not a center — pick the end you'd actually pull your tape from. L means left end of the run to the left edge of the plate; R means right end to the right edge. Same idea vertically: floor to the bottom edge, or floor to the top. Light fixtures are the exception: measure to their center, both ways. Tap a box count to lay it out.
